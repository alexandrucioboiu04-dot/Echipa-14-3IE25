# Zombie Tsunami - PC Edition

A fun zombie horde game inspired by the classic mobile game, optimized for PC play! Lead your zombie horde and convert people to grow your army!

## How to Play

1. **Open `index.html`** in your web browser
2. Click "Start Game" to begin
3. Lead your zombie horde and convert people to zombies!

## Controls

- **Space Bar** or **W** or **Up Arrow**: Make zombies jump
- **Mouse**: Click buttons for menu navigation
- **Map Movement**: Automatically scrolls right - no manual movement needed!

## Game Features

### Core Gameplay
- **Zombie Horde**: Start with 3 zombies and grow your horde
- **Convert People**: Touch people with zombies to convert them
- **Destroy Objects**: Jump zombies onto cars to destroy them
- **Power-ups**: Collect question mark boxes for special abilities
- **Progressive Difficulty**: More challenges as you progress

### Power-ups (Question Mark Boxes)
- **Ninja Mode**: Your zombies become ninjas (black color) for 10 seconds
- **Tsunami Mode**: Create a destructive tsunami wave for 8 seconds
- **Magnet Mode**: Attract nearby people to your position

### Game Elements
- **Zombie Horde**: Green zombies that move in formation (no human player)
- **People**: Pink characters that run away and can be converted
- **Objects**: Gray cars that can be destroyed by jumping zombies onto them
- **Power-up Boxes**: Orange boxes with question marks
- **Tsunami Effect**: Blue wave that destroys everything in its path

### Scoring System
- **Convert People**: +50 points per person
- **Destroy Objects**: +100 points per object
- **Tsunami Destruction**: +200 points per object, +100 per person
- **Survival Time**: Continuous points based on game speed

## Game Mechanics

### Zombie Horde Management
- Start with 3 zombies in formation
- Zombies move automatically with the scrolling map
- Each converted person adds one zombie to your horde
- Zombies stay in a fixed horizontal formation

### Object Destruction
- Cars and obstacles have health bars (4 health points)
- Jump zombies onto objects to damage them
- Objects are destroyed when health reaches 0
- Multiple zombies can damage objects simultaneously

### People Behavior
- People run away when zombies get close
- Converted people immediately join your zombie horde
- People spawn continuously throughout the game

### Special Abilities
- **Ninja Mode**: Zombies can pass through objects
- **Tsunami Mode**: Creates a destructive wave you can control
- **Magnet Mode**: Attracts people toward your position

### Map Movement
- The map continuously scrolls to the right
- No manual horizontal movement required
- All game elements move with the camera
- Creates an endless runner experience

## Technical Details

- **Engine**: HTML5 Canvas with JavaScript
- **Graphics**: 2D pixel-style graphics
- **Physics**: Custom collision detection and zombie jumping mechanics
- **Camera**: Continuous horizontal scrolling
- **Responsive**: Optimized for PC screens (1200x600)
- **Multiplayer**: Multiple players based on p2p

## Tips for High Scores

1. **Time Your Jumps**: Jump zombies onto objects to destroy them efficiently
2. **Grow Your Horde**: Convert as many people as possible early
3. **Use Power-ups Wisely**: Save tsunami mode for large groups
4. **Watch Object Health**: Objects show remaining health with red/green bars
5. **Stay in Formation**: Zombies automatically maintain their formation
6. **Tsunami Strategy**: Use tsunami mode to clear large areas quickly

## Game Objectives

- **Primary Goal**: Grow your zombie horde as large as possible
- **Secondary Goal**: Achieve the highest score
- **Challenge**: Survive as long as possible while expanding your horde

## Key Differences from Original

- **No Human Player**: You control only the zombie horde
- **Jumping Only**: No horizontal movement controls
- **Auto-Scrolling**: Map moves automatically to the right
- **Object Interaction**: Jump zombies onto objects to destroy them
- **Formation Movement**: Zombies stay in a fixed formation
